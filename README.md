TCPipe is a real estate transaction website for transaction coordinators and real estate brokers. It helps manage all the deals and transaction.

We have a dashboard(index.html), it gives you a summary of everything, consisting of 
-items closing in 7 Days
-Overdue Deadlines
-High Risk Deals
-Past-Due Closings
-Active Deals

New deal page (add.html): where you can add transactions and input various types of info given in the fields.
-Basic Info
-Parties
-Dates & Deadlines
-Location
-Financials
-Title & Lender
-Risk & Notes
-Milestones

Transactions page lists everything you inputted from the New deal page, it will give the 
-File ID	
-Address	
-Stage	
-Closing Date	
-Days to Close	
-Risk	
-Actions
You can filter the transactions based on What state you are at and the risk level.
And in this page you can edit, delete, and compose an email
