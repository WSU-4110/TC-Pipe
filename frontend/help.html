Help guides

Step-by-step instructions for the main parts of the app.

Registering and logging in

Account registration and login aren’t available yet. For now you can use TC Pipeline without signing in.

Your profile details (name, email, brokerage) are saved in this browser only — see Profile settings below.

When accounts are added, this guide will explain how to register and sign in.

The dashboard

The Dashboard is your daily overview of every active deal.

  • Stat cards show deals closing in the next 7 days, overdue deadlines, high-risk deals, closings that have already passed, and your active deals.
  • Needs Attention lists overdue deadlines, deals whose closing date has passed, and anything marked as a blocker.
  • Upcoming Deadlines shows open deadlines in the next 30 days. A deadline disappears once its milestone is checked off.
  • Active Deals is the full table. Search by file ID, address or client, filter by risk, or change the sort order.

Use Refresh to reload the latest data. Closed and terminated deals are hidden.

Creating and editing transactions

To create a transaction

  1. Click New Deal in the menu.
  2. Enter a File ID. It’s required and must be unique.
  3. Fill in the property, people, dates, prices and milestones you know. Everything else can be left blank and added later.
  4. Click the save button at the bottom of the form.

To edit or delete one

  1. Open Transactions (or use Edit in the dashboard table).
  2. Click Edit on the deal, change what you need and save. The File ID can’t be changed.
  3. To remove a deal, click Delete and confirm.

Tick the milestone checkboxes as each step is completed. The dashboard uses them to work out risk and which deadlines are still open.

Analyzing an investment property
  1. Open Analyzer in the menu.
  2. Enter the purchase price, total cash invested and expected monthly rent.
  3. Enter the vacancy rate and management fee as percentages, and your yearly property taxes, insurance, maintenance, other expenses and mortgage payments.
  4. Click the analyze button.

You’ll see the gross rent multiplier (GRM), gross yield, cap rate and cash-on-cash return for the property.

Profile settings
  1. Open Settings in the menu.
  2. Enter your full name, email, phone, company or brokerage, title and LinkedIn address.
  3. Click save.

Your first name is used in the dashboard greeting, and your details are added to the signature of emails you create from the Transactions page. They’re stored in this browser only, so you’ll need to enter them again on another device.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Why is my deal marked High risk?

Hover over the risk label in the dashboard to see the reason. In general a deal is High when its closing date has passed, or it closes within 7 days with 2 or more open milestones. It’s Medium when it closes within 14 days with an open milestone, has an overdue deadline, or has no closing date.

If someone set a risk level by hand on the deal, that level is used instead and the label says manual.

What counts as an overdue deadline?

A deadline (earnest money, inspection, appraisal or financing) is overdue when its date has passed and the matching milestone isn’t checked off yet. Tick the milestone on the deal and the deadline clears.

Why does the dashboard say “Good evening, there”?

No name is saved yet. Add your full name in Settings and the greeting will use your first name.

What does “Data to fix” on the dashboard mean?

Some deals are missing information, such as a property address or closing date, or have a date that isn’t valid. Open the deal with the Edit link in that list and fill in the missing details.

Where is my information stored?

Transactions are stored on the TC Pipeline server. Your profile settings are stored in your own browser.

Can I email my clients from TC Pipeline?

Yes. On Transactions, click Email on a deal and choose a template. The subject and message are filled in from the deal. You can then copy the text or open it in Gmail or Outlook.

I clicked Save and nothing happened

Refresh the page and check whether your change is there. If it isn’t, the server may be unreachable. Try again in a minute, and if it keeps happening please report a bug so we can look into it.
